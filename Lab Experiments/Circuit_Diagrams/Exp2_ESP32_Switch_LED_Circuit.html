Interfacing Push Button with ESP32 for LED Control

Circuit Connections

  • ESP32 GPIO2 ──→ 220Ω ──→ LED Anode (+)
  • LED Cathode (−) ──→ GND
  • ESP32 GPIO4 ──→ Push-button terminal 1
  • Push-button terminal 2 ──→ GND
  • GPIO4 uses INPUT_PULLUP (no external resistor needed)
