Experiment 3 — Circuit Diagram

Interfacing 16x2 LCD with ESP32 using I2C

Circuit Connections

  • ESP32 GPIO21 (SDA) ──→ I2C LCD SDA
  • ESP32 GPIO22 (SCL) ──→ I2C LCD SCL
  • ESP32 3.3V / 5V ──→ I2C LCD VCC
  • ESP32 GND ──→ I2C LCD GND
  • I2C Address: 0x27 (or 0x3F — check with I2C scanner)
