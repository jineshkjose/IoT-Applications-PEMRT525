Experiment 5 — Circuit Diagram

Interfacing Push Button with Raspberry Pi for LED Control

Circuit Connections

  • RPi GPIO17 (Pin 11) ──→ 220Ω ──→ LED Anode (+)
  • LED Cathode (−) ──→ GND (Pin 6)
  • RPi GPIO18 (Pin 12) ──→ Push-button terminal 1
  • Push-button terminal 2 ──→ GND (Pin 14)
  • GPIO18 uses GPIO.PUD_UP (internal pull-up, ~50kΩ)
